Read it from the driveway

HVAC is a driveway trade. The van sits in front of the house for a tune-up or a full changeout, and every neighbor who walks a dog past it reads the door. What they should see is one strong mark and a name — not a sun, a snowflake, a house, and a swoosh crowded into the same panel. The companies that own a market usually own one simple shape.

Your logo also lives small: on the sticker you leave on the air handler, on the magnet on the fridge, on the corner of a maintenance-plan invoice. A filled badge stays legible at sticker size; a thin line drawing of a heat pump does not.

Six HVAC badges

Fan Circle is the condenser fan seen from above — the trade’s cleanest icon, and it works one-color. Heat & Cool Badge splits flame-side and cool-side in one shield, ideal if you sell both seasons. Duct Elbow speaks to sheet-metal-proud shops that fabricate their own trunk and branch. Thermostat Dial reads comfort and controls, Condenser Unit is the equipment itself — the box you set on the pad — and Airflow Arrows is the abstract option for ventilation and IAQ work.

Tap your badge and it loads in the full maker; add the company name and a tagline like HEATING · COOLING · IAQ if you run one.

Questions

Is the HVAC logo maker completely free?

Yes — design and download the PNG with no account, no watermark, and nothing to cancel later. It’s one of the Digital Foreman Suite’s free contractor tools.

Do I own what I make here?

Fully. The badge renders on your own device and downloads straight to it — nothing is uploaded to us, so there is no copy of your logo anywhere but with you. Registering the company name is a trademark attorney’s job.

What files do I get for stickers and uniforms?

Free: a 1200×600 transparent PNG plus a white-background copy — fine for digital printing and equipment stickers. Uniform embroiderers and sign shops ask for vector: the Suite Pro SVG (lettering outlined) plus one-color and reverse versions is exactly that.
