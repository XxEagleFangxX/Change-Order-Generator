Clean lines from the street

Siding is curb appeal for a living — you sell straight courses and tight corners, and the whole neighborhood can inspect your work from the sidewalk. Your logo gets judged by the same eye. Crooked, cluttered marks undercut a trade whose entire pitch is clean lines; one bold shape with level lettering underneath makes the point before your ladder is off the rack.

And this trade’s branding does real mileage: the yard sign stays up through a week of tear-off and hang, the trailer sits out front, and the wrap on the box truck passes a thousand houses a day between jobs.
