A logo that holds up like your flatwork

Concrete is a trade where the work itself is the proof — a driveway with straight control joints and an even broom finish sells the next three driveways on the block. Your logo should hold to the same standard: flat, solid, no wasted ornament. One heavy mark and a name that reads off a truck door or the side of a form truck at thirty feet.

What kills concrete logos is clutter — a mixer, a skyline, a shovel, and a swoosh all at once. Nobody can read that from the street while your crew is screeding. Pick one shape that says concrete and let it carry the door.

What the six concrete badges mean

The Mixer Drum is the obvious flag for ready-mix-heavy outfits. The Finishing Trowel and Screeded Slab speak straight to flatwork crews — the trowel every finisher has worn smooth, the screed line moving across a fresh pour. Stamped Square is for decorative work: stamped patios, borders, exposed aggregate. Footing Section shows a footing-and-stem-wall profile for foundation contractors, and Rebar Grid is the tied mat every inspector photographs before the pour.

Tap the one that matches your book of work and the full maker opens with it selected. From there: company name, EST. year if you’ve earned one, colors, done.

Pour it into your paperwork

Concrete-gray and charcoal palettes look right for this trade, with one safety-orange or steel-blue accent. Two tones survive being printed small on an invoice and big on a jobsite banner without falling apart.

And the paperwork matters more in concrete than most trades — add-ons like extra yardage, pump time, or a thicker slab edge need signatures before the truck rolls. Your new logo drops straight onto the free letterhead studio, so change orders and bids go out under your mark, not a blank header.

Questions

Is the concrete logo maker free, or a trial?

Free, full stop. Design the badge, download the PNG — no account, no watermark, no time limit. It’s part of the free Digital Foreman Suite toolset for contractors.

Do I own the finished logo?

Yes. The whole design is drawn on your device and downloads straight to it — nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored on our end. Your name, your mark. Trademark registration for the name itself is attorney territory.

What about vector files for the truck lettering shop?

The free PNG (1200×600, transparent plus white-background) handles most printing. Vinyl and lettering shops want vector — Suite Pro adds a real SVG export with outlined lettering, hi-res sizes, and one-color/reverse versions built for cut vinyl.
