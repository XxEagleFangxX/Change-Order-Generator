Seen from the property line

Fence and deck work is neighborhood work. The yard sign at the end of a fresh cedar run, the truck parked out front for three days, the trailer stacked with pickets — that’s where the next job comes from. A logo for this trade has to read across a lawn: one confident shape, a name in tall letters, and nothing else competing.

The good news is the work itself is graphic. Pickets in a row, the profile of a post cap, a pergola’s rafter tails — these are already logos waiting to be cleaned up. That’s exactly what the six badges above are.

Six badges for fence and deck outfits

Picket Run is the classic dog-eared fence line — instantly legible on a magnet. Slat Panel reads horizontal-modern for crews building privacy screens and board-on-board. Deck & Rail shows deck boards meeting a guardrail, the two things every deck inspection checks. Post Cap makes a tidy badge out of the detail customers actually notice, Arbor Gate flags gates and entry arbors, and Pergola Frame is for the outdoor-living side — pergolas, pavilions, shade structures.
