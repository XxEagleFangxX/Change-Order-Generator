Questions

Is this logo maker really free?

Yes. Design and download free — no account, no watermark, no catch. It's part of the Digital Foreman Suite, three free apps for contractors, and it feeds straight into the free letterhead studio.

Do I own the logo I make?

Yes — it's your name and your choices, and the file downloads straight to your device. Nothing is uploaded to us and we keep no copy, so there's nothing for anyone else to claim. For trademark questions on a business name itself, that's one for a trademark attorney.

What file formats do I get?

Free: a 1200×600 transparent PNG plus a white-background copy — sharp on letterheads, invoices, cards, and profiles. Suite Pro adds a true vector SVG with the lettering converted to outlines, a high-res size pack, and one-color + reverse versions for embroidery, signage, and shirt printing.

Can I put this logo on my paperwork?

That's the point. One tap sends it to the letterhead studio, where it goes on every change order, bid leveling sheet, and punch list PDF — preview free, Suite Pro puts it on the real documents.

Can I take this to a shirt or sign shop?

Yes. The free PNG covers most print-on-demand shirt sites. Pro's SVG is the vector file printers and sign shops actually ask for — no fonts needed on their end, scales to a banner without going fuzzy.
