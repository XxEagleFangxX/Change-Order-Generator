Pattern is your portfolio

Flooring is the rare trade whose work is literally a graphic — herringbone, chevron, running bond, all patterns before they’re products. A flooring logo should steal from that. One clean pattern block with your name under it tells a customer you think in layouts, which is exactly what they’re hiring: the installer who plans the room from the doorway sight line instead of starting in a corner and hoping.

It also has to shrink. Your mark will live on an invoice line, a van door, and a lawn sign at three very different sizes — a bold filled block survives all of them, a delicate wood-grain illustration doesn’t.

Six flooring badges

Herringbone Planks is the premium-install flag — anyone who’s racked one knows the layout work behind it. Chevron Parquet is its sharper cousin. Tongue & Groove shows the joint profile itself, a badge only floor people would draw and exactly why it works. Tile Meets Plank is the transition detail — ideal if you install everything from LVP to porcelain. Roll-Out Wave covers carpet and sheet-vinyl crews, and Staircase Steps flags stair work: treads, risers, nosings — the jobs with the best margins.

Lay it into your documents

Warm wood-tone accents over a dark main read flooring immediately; navy and brass reads showroom. Either way, verify the one-color mode — the badge should hold its pattern from punched gaps alone, the way a good layout holds without grout lines drawn on.

Then the paperwork nobody warns you about: the measure-and-quote, the change order when the subfloor needs leveling compound before a single plank goes down, the acclimation note you want in writing. The logo feeds the free letterhead studio, so every document matches the van.

Questions

Free flooring logos — what’s the catch?

No catch. Design and download the PNG free with no account and no watermark. The maker is part of the Digital Foreman Suite, which makes its money on an optional one-time Pro upgrade, not on your logo.

Do I own the logo afterward?

Yes — it never leaves your device except when you share it. Nothing uploads to us and we keep no copy, so the mark is entirely yours. Protecting the trading name itself means a trademark filing.

What formats are included — anything for signage?

Free: 1200×600 transparent PNG plus a white-background copy. For cut vinyl and large signage, shops want vector — the Suite Pro pack includes a real SVG (lettering outlined), hi-res exports, and one-color/reverse files.

Can I put it on quotes and change orders?

Yes. One tap sends it into the free letterhead studio; Suite Pro ($29.99 once) puts it on the working PDFs — change orders, bid leveling sheets, punch lists — across all three apps.
