Green that doesn’t look generic

Every landscaping logo in town is a green swoosh with a leaf on it, which is exactly why yours shouldn’t be. The trucks that stand out in a maintenance-day convoy carry one strong, specific mark — a tree with real presence, stripes that look like an actual finished lawn — with the company name big enough to read as the trailer rolls past.

Landscaping is also a route business: the same neighbors see your trailer every week. A consistent, confident badge compounds that exposure; a cluttered one just becomes part of the noise.

Six landscaping badges

Tree Circle is the anchor mark — a full-canopy tree in a ring, classic enough for an established company. Mower Stripes puts the finished product on the badge: the alternating light-dark bands of a properly striped lawn. Leaf & Blade pairs foliage with turf for full-service crews. Paver Path flags hardscape — walkways, patios, the running-bond layout under every paver job. Wall & Planting is the retaining-wall-and-bed combo that defines design-build scope, and Sun & Turf gives irrigation and lawn-health outfits a bright, optimistic mark.

From trailer gate to invoice

You don’t have to run green — a deep forest main with a warm stone accent reads high-end design-build, and navy with lime reads sharp and modern. Check the badge in one-color mode too; it’ll end up on black-and-white service agreements sooner or later.
