What belongs on a plumbing van

A plumbing van is a rolling billboard that parks in driveways all day, and most of them waste it — either a bare white panel or a cartoon mascot holding a plunger. The sweet spot is simpler: one solid mark that says licensed trade, a company name readable from the neighbor’s porch, and a phone number. Homeowners calling about a water heater at 7 a.m. want competent, not comic.

Service work also means your logo shows up small — on an invoice left on the counter, on a shirt pocket, on a magnet stuck to the customer’s fridge. A heavy filled badge survives all three; a thin sketch of a faucet doesn’t.

Six plumbing badges that aren’t a cartoon drip

The Pipe Wrench is the trade’s oldest badge and still the strongest. P-Trap Run turns the fitting every plumber has assembled a thousand times into a clean mark — people in the trade recognize it instantly, which is the point. Valve Wheel and Cross Fitting read mechanical and commercial; Water Heater is the flag if changeouts and service plans are your bread and butter; Faucet Drop covers fixture and remodel work without going cartoonish.

From the van door to the invoice

Questions

Is the plumbing logo maker free or freemium?

The maker and the PNG download are simply free — no account, no watermark, no upsell wall in front of the file. Suite Pro exists only if you later want vector files and branded PDFs.

Do I own the logo outright?

Yes. It’s generated on your device, downloaded to your device, and never sent to us — there’s no copy to license back to you. The business name itself is a trademark-office matter if you want it registered.

What does the shirt printer need, and do you provide it?

Embroiderers and screen printers ask for vector art. The free PNG works for digital print, but the Suite Pro pack adds a genuine SVG with outlined lettering plus one-color and reverse versions — exactly what they mean when they say “send us the vector.”

Can it go on my service invoices and change orders?

Yes — one tap pushes it into the free letterhead studio, and Suite Pro prints it on every PDF from the three DFS apps: change orders, bid comparisons, punch lists.
